# La Chasse Galerie: Doctrine

> **PROPOSED** by the Doctrine upkeep (r97) on 2026-10-09. It binds nothing until Simon
> approves it on the board's Doctrine page.

**D1. Online is friends playing from their own homes: one hero per machine, and a player hosts the game.**
No couch of pads brought online from one machine, no matchmaking with strangers.

**D2. Anyone gets in with what they already hold: any key, any pad, or a browser tab.**
No screen or way to play may need one device only (no mouse-only menu, no join that needs an install).

**D3. Every player gets the same game, whatever their screen or machine.**
Window size, frame rate or browser never change how the game plays.

**D4. A run is a song: it has a start, a ride and an ending, and always a clear way back to the menu.**

**D5. We host only what is small (the lobby, the relay, the page); the game itself runs on a player's machine.**
No dedicated game servers, no monthly bill that grows with players.
